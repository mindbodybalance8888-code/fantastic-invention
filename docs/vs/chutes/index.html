Budget for the route and the burst

A monthly allowance does not establish how much work can run in a short session. Check both the applicable time windows and the billing fallback before starting an automated workload. Neither service is benchmarked here for task quality or speed.

Read LU's cloud capabilities and restrictions before buying. Hosted requests leave your machine, and image and video generation have content restrictions. Local inference depends on your hardware and backend.
