LU Cloud and SillyHost

These services sell different things. SillyHost hosts a SillyTavern instance; LU Cloud provides hosted inference through its apps and API. Instance hosting and inference usage are not interchangeable purchases.

Checked September 9, 2026. Plans can change. Follow the sources and recheck before purchasing.

Choose the service your workflow needs

Start with whether you need a managed frontend instance, inference access, or both. Check provider limits and billing separately from hosting. This page does not establish interoperability between the services, measure performance or rank token prices.

Read LU's cloud capabilities and restrictions before buying. Hosted requests leave your machine, and image and video generation have content restrictions. Local inference depends on your hardware and backend.
